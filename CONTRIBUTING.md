Keep PRs small, simple and about one thing.

PRs with more than a few dozen or hundred lines of code are very unlikely
to be merged. When in doubt, please first open a ticket to have a quick
discussion.

Don't throw LLM generated walls of text at me, such issues and PRs
will be closed immediately.

Keep in mind that LLM code generation in the sokol headers themselves
is forbidden (see AGENTS.md for LLM usage rules).

Short and to-the-point LLM assisted bug reports are still welcome. For
a good example see: https://github.com/floooh/sokol/issues/1586

The best PRs are small bugfixes, those are usually merged on the
same or next day.
